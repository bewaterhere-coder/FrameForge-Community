# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our commitment / 我们的承诺

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and constructive community. / 我们致力于建立尊重、包容、建设性的社区。

## Expected behavior / 预期行为

- Be respectful and considerate. Critique ideas and technical work, not people.
- Welcome different experience levels, backgrounds and approaches.
- Give actionable, evidence-based feedback.
- Respect project ownership, licenses, privacy and confidentiality.
- Use community spaces for project-related discussion; avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks and unwanted contact.

- 尊重他人，讨论技术方案而非攻击个人。
- 接纳不同经验、背景和技术路线。
- 提供具体、可执行且有证据的反馈。
- 尊重项目所有权、许可证、隐私与保密要求。
- 禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、人身攻击及未经欢迎的持续联系。

## Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受行为

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, and submitting content that violates third-party rights are not acceptable.

骚扰、歧视性言论、威胁、人肉搜索、公开私人信息、恶意扰乱社区，以及提交侵犯第三方权利的内容均不可接受。

## Enforcement / 执行

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or block accounts depending on severity. Reports should be sent privately through the repository's available security/contact channel. Do not publish sensitive details in a public issue.

维护者可根据严重程度编辑或删除违规内容、拒绝贡献、暂时限制参与或屏蔽账号。请通过仓库可用的私密安全/联系渠道报告，不要在公开 Issue 中披露敏感细节。

## Scope / 适用范围

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other spaces operated for the project.

本准则适用于仓库 Issue、Pull Request、代码评审、讨论区及项目运营的其他空间。
